How this works
A scheduled job collects headlines from all sources every 3 hours. Every day at 07:00 PHT it groups headlines that describe the same story, using the uncommon words they share. Stories are ranked by how many different outlets covered them, whether they touch market-moving topics (⚡), how many days they kept running, and how strongly bullish or bearish the coverage is. No AI is involved.
To pin a story by hand, add it to data/pinned.json as {"title","link","summary","image","source","date"}. Pins stay up for 7 days from their date, or until an until date if you set one. To republish right away, run the workflow from the GitHub Actions tab.
